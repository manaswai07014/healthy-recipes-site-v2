# White Wine Shrimp and Scallop Pasta with Lemon-Parmesan Sauce

Juicy shrimp and sweet scallops meet tender pasta in a glossy white wine sauce, brightened with lemon and finished with Parmesan. The wine reduces until fragrant.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 225 g spaghetti
- 300 g peeled, deveined raw shrimp
- 150 g sea scallops, muscle removed
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 60 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 20 g finely grated Parmesan
- 15 g chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a rolling boil. Cook the spaghetti for 8-10 minutes, stirring during the first 2 minutes, until al dente. Reserve 120 ml of the starchy cooking water, then drain.
2. Step 2: Pat the shrimp and scallops thoroughly dry. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in one layer and cook for 45-60 seconds per side, until opaque and just firm. Transfer them to a plate.
3. Step 3: Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the same pan. Lower the heat to medium and cook the onion for 3-4 minutes, until soft and translucent, without browning. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and stir for 30 seconds, until the garlic smells fragrant rather than burnt.
4. Step 4: Pour in the white wine and increase the heat to medium-high. Simmer for 3-4 minutes, scraping the pan bottom, until the sharp alcohol aroma softens and the liquid reduces by roughly half. Add the chicken stock and simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Step 5: Add the drained spaghetti and 60 ml reserved pasta water to the pan. Toss vigorously for 1-2 minutes, loosening the strands and creating a lightly glossy sauce. Add the cooked shrimp and scallops and toss gently for 30 seconds, heating them through to 63°C.
6. Step 6: Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the lemon zest, 1 tbsp lemon juice, Parmesan, and parsley. Season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately in warm bowls, adding more pasta water by the tablespoon if the sauce needs loosening.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 476 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 10 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

Dry the seafood before searing so its surface caramelizes instead of steaming. Cook the shrimp and scallops separately from the wine sauce, then add them back briefly to keep their textures tender; use a thermometer to confirm the seafood reaches 63°C. For a lower-sodium sauce, replace the chicken stock with 60 ml dry white wine plus an additional 30 ml water. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 1 day, reheating with a splash of water over low heat.
