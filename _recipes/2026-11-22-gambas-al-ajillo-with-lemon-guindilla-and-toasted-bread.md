# Gambas al Ajillo with Lemon, Guindilla, and Toasted Bread

Raw shrimp sizzle in olive oil with sliced garlic, lemon, and a whisper of guindilla, releasing a fragrant aroma. A splash of wine creates a glossy sauce.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 450 g raw peeled shrimp, deveined, tails on
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 12 g garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp dried guindilla chili flakes
- 60 ml dry white wine
- 60 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 15 g flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 30 g whole-grain bread, cut into 4 pieces
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the shrimp completely dry with paper towels and season with the sea salt and black pepper. Moisture on the surface is the main obstacle to browning, so take an extra minute to blot them evenly.
2. Step 2: Place the bread pieces on a baking sheet and toast at 200°C for 5-7 minutes, turning once, until the edges are crisp and the centers remain slightly springy. Transfer to a plate.
3. Step 3: Heat the extra-virgin olive oil and avocado oil in a wide sauté pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and guindilla; cook for 45-60 seconds, stirring, until the garlic turns pale gold and smells sweet rather than raw. Do not let it brown, because dark garlic becomes bitter.
4. Step 4: Raise the heat to medium-high and spread the shrimp in one layer. Sear for 60-90 seconds per side, just until the flesh turns opaque and firm. The tails should stay attached; overcooking makes the shrimp rubbery.
5. Step 5: Add the wine and scrape the browned bits from the pan with a wooden spoon. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until the liquid reduces by roughly half and no raw alcohol smell remains.
6. Step 6: Stir in the chicken stock, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Return the shrimp to the pan and simmer for 2 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken to a light, glossy coating. The shrimp must reach an internal temperature of 63°C.
7. Step 7: Fold in the spinach and parsley, cooking for 30-45 seconds until the spinach wilts. Remove the pan from the heat so the residual heat does not dry the shrimp.
8. Step 8: Spoon the shrimp and sauce over the toasted bread, scatter with additional parsley, and serve immediately with lemon wedges. The sauce is best while fluid enough to moisten the bread.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more restrained heat level, use 1/2 teaspoon of guindilla and add the remaining flakes at the end. If the shrimp release liquid during cooking, drain it before returning them to the pan, then reduce the sauce separately until it coats the back of a spoon. The dish is best served immediately, but leftover shrimp and sauce can be refrigerated for up to 2 days and gently reheated with a teaspoon of water; toast fresh bread separately so it stays crisp.
