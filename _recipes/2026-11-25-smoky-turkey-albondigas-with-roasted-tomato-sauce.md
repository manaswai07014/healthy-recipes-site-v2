# Smoky Turkey Albondigas with Roasted Tomato Sauce

Tender turkey meatballs simmer in a smoky tomato sauce scented with smoked paprika. Browning them first adds savory depth while keeping the weeknight meal lean.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 500 g lean ground turkey
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 40 g stale whole-grain bread, torn into crumbs
- 20 g finely grated manchego cheese
- 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 tsp smoked sweet paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 200 ml water or low-sodium stock
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 250 g whole-wheat couscous

## Method

1. Step 1: Put the bread into a small bowl, pour over 80 ml hot water and leave for 5 minutes. Squeeze out the excess liquid, then combine it with the turkey, egg, manchego, onion, garlic, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper. Mix with a light hand for 1 minute until evenly bound, avoiding a dense mixture.
2. Step 2: Shape the mixture into 12 compact meatballs, about 35 g each. Warm the olive oil in a wide frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs in one layer and brown for 3-4 minutes, turning them until each side is golden. They should sizzle on contact and release easily from the pan.
3. Step 3: Stir the tomato paste into the pan and cook for 30 seconds, until it darkens to brick red. Add the chopped tomatoes, 200 ml water or stock and oregano. Bring to a gentle simmer, then cover and cook for 12-15 minutes. The sauce should thicken and the meatballs should reach 74°C internally.
4. Step 4: Stir the spinach through the sauce in handfuls and cook for 2 minutes until wilted. Taste and adjust the salt. Keep the sauce at a low simmer while you prepare the couscous.
5. Step 5: Put the whole-wheat couscous and 250 ml water in a saucepan with a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, cover and remove from the heat. Leave for 8 minutes, then fluff with a fork, separating the grains and loosening any clumps.
6. Step 6: Spoon the couscous into bowls and top with 4 meatballs and their tomato sauce. Scatter with parsley and serve while the sauce is steaming and the meatballs are hot throughout.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 498 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 39 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 7 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more concentrated sauce, simmer it uncovered for the final 5 minutes after the meatballs are cooked through; the tomatoes will reduce to a glossy consistency without adding fat. If manchego is unavailable, use another firm Spanish cheese such as Monterey Jack. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 days, reheating until the meatballs reach 74°C.
