# Gambas al Ajillo with Toasted Garlic and Parsley Oil

Shell-on shrimp sizzle in olive oil with sliced garlic and a restrained chili heat, finishing with parsley, sherry, and a lemony sheen.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 300 g raw peeled, deveined shrimp, tails on
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp dry white wine or dry sherry
- 12 g thinly sliced garlic
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 60 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 240 g cooked chickpeas, drained
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 tbsp toasted sliced almonds

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the shrimp thoroughly dry with paper towels and season with the salt and black pepper. Moisture on the surface is the enemy of browning, so this brief drying step gives the shrimp a lightly seared exterior rather than a steamed texture.
2. Step 2: Place the chickpeas in a small saucepan with the chicken stock and half the parsley. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-high heat, then reduce the heat and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring once, until the chickpeas are warm and beginning to soften.
3. Step 3: Heat the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 45 to 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until the garlic smells sweet and turns pale gold; remove it immediately if it darkens, because bitter garlic will dominate the finished sauce.
4. Step 4: Increase the heat to medium-high and arrange the shrimp in a single layer. Cook for 90 seconds without moving them, allowing the underside to set. Turn the shrimp and cook for another 60 to 90 seconds, until the flesh is opaque, firm, and just pink, with the tail shells bright red. Transfer the shrimp to a clean plate.
5. Step 5: Lower the skillet heat to medium. Pour in the wine or sherry and scrape the browned bits from the pan with the back of a spoon. Simmer for 1 minute until the alcohol has cooked off, then add the chickpeas, stock, and remaining parsley. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, until the liquid lightly coats the chickpeas and the shrimp.
6. Step 6: Return the shrimp to the skillet for 30 seconds to warm through, then remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the lemon juice and cilantro. Taste before adding more salt, adjusting with a small splash of stock if the sauce needs loosening.
7. Step 7: Divide the chickpeas and shrimp among 3 bowls. Spoon over the pan sauce and finish with the toasted almonds, adding crunch and a lightly roasted aroma.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 428 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 15 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For the most even cooking, choose shrimp of similar size and avoid crowding the skillet; a second batch is better than a crowded pan that steams. The sauce can be prepared up to 2 hours ahead, but hold the shrimp separately and add them during the final 2 minutes so they retain their springy texture. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 24 hours, reheating gently in the sauce until the shrimp are piping hot throughout.
