# Herbed Italian Stuffed Bell Peppers with Ground Turkey and Bulgur

Roasted peppers cradle herbed turkey, tomato, and nutty bulgur, finished with melted mozzarella. The filling stays moist while the peppers soften.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 medium bell peppers, tops and seeds removed
- 300 g lean ground turkey
- 1/2 cup dry bulgur wheat, rinsed
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup finely chopped onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup crushed no-salt-added tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried basil
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 30 g part-skim mozzarella, grated
- 10 g fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat the oven to 200°C. Place the bulgur and water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes; the grains should be tender with no hard center.
2. Step 2: While the bulgur cooks, halve the peppers lengthways and remove the seeds and inner ribs. Arrange cut-side up in a 23 by 33 cm baking dish. Brush lightly with half the olive oil and season with a small pinch of salt.
3. Step 3: Heat the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes until translucent and sweet-smelling. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and stir for 30 seconds.
4. Step 4: Add the ground turkey. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking it into small crumbles with a wooden spoon, until no pink remains and the meat reaches 74°C. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, black pepper, and remaining salt. Simmer for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Step 5: Fold the cooked bulgur and parsley through the turkey mixture. Divide it evenly among the pepper halves, pressing it firmly into the cavities. Top each with grated mozzarella.
6. Step 6: Bake for 18 to 22 minutes at 200°C, until the peppers are tender and the cheese is bubbling with lightly browned edges. Rest for 5 minutes before serving so the hot filling settles.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 34 g
- Fat: 15 g
- Fiber: 7 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

Choose peppers of similar size so they finish cooking at the same rate. For a saucier version, add 30 ml of water to the baking dish before roasting; the bulgur will absorb it and stay moist. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 days, reheating until steaming throughout.
