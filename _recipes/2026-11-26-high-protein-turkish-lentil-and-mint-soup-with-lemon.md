# High-Protein Turkish Lentil and Mint Soup with Lemon

Silky red lentils collapse into a warmly spiced broth, finished with fresh mint and lemon for a bright, cooling finish.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium carrot, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried mint
- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 800 ml low-sodium vegetable stock
- 400 ml water
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 15 g fresh mint leaves, torn
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat the olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot with a pinch of salt, then cook for 6-8 minutes until the edges soften and the onion turns translucent. Stir occasionally so the vegetables soften rather than brown.
2. Step 2: Add the garlic, cumin, and dried mint. Cook for 45-60 seconds, stirring, until the spices release a warm aroma and the garlic is fragrant but not colored.
3. Step 3: Add the rinsed lentils, vegetable stock, and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to a gentle simmer. Skim any foam from the surface and cook uncovered for 20-25 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until the lentils have broken down and the soup is thick enough to coat a spoon.
4. Step 4: Remove the pan from the heat and let the soup cool for 3-5 minutes. Whisk in half the Greek yogurt, then add the lemon juice, fresh mint, salt, and pepper. Taste and adjust the seasoning: the lemon should sharpen the mint without making the broth sour.
5. Step 5: Ladle into warmed bowls. Finish each serving with the remaining yogurt, a few torn mint leaves, and a thin drizzle of olive oil. Serve immediately while the soup is steaming and the lentils are silky.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 408 kcal
- Protein: 24 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 12 g
- Fiber: 15 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a thicker soup, simmer for the full 25 minutes and use a potato masher to press some lentils against the side of the pan; no blender is needed. If the soup loosens after standing, warm it gently with a splash of stock. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 days, reheating only until steaming; add fresh mint after reheating to preserve its aroma.
