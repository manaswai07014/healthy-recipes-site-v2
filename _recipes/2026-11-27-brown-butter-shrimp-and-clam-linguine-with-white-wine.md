# Brown Butter Shrimp and Clam Linguine with White Wine

Silky linguine meets sweet clams, firm shrimp, and nutty brown butter in a brisk white-wine sauce, brightened with lemon and parsley.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 225 g linguine
- 300 g raw peeled shrimp, deveined
- 450 g fresh clams in shells, scrubbed
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 60 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium shallot, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 30 g flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 30 g Parmesan, finely grated

## Method

1. Step 1: Put a large pot of lightly salted water on the heat and bring it to a rolling boil. Add the linguine and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it is just shy of al dente. Scoop out 120 ml of pasta water before draining; the starch will help the sauce cling.
2. Step 2: While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a wide sauté pan over medium heat. Add the shallot and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened and translucent, without browning. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes for 45 seconds, stirring until the garlic smells fragrant rather than burnt.
3. Step 3: Increase the heat to medium-high and add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook for 2 minutes, turning once, until the exterior turns pink and the centers are just opaque. Transfer the shrimp to a plate; they will finish cooking in the sauce.
4. Step 4: Add the clams to the same pan and pour in the white wine. Cover and cook for 4-6 minutes, shaking the pan gently, until the clam shells open. Discard any clams that remain closed. Lift out the cooked clams, keeping their broth, and set them aside.
5. Step 5: Lower the heat to medium. Add the butter and let it foam, then turn off the burner and let it brown for 1-2 minutes until the milk solids turn hazelnut brown and smell nutty. Whisk in the reserved clam broth and chicken stock.
6. Step 6: Add the drained linguine and shrimp to the pan. Toss vigorously for 1 minute, adding reserved pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce looks glossy and lightly coats the noodles. Return the clams to the pan and toss once.
7. Step 7: Remove the pan from the heat. Fold in the parsley, lemon zest, lemon juice, Parmesan, salt, and black pepper. Taste before adding more salt: the clam broth and Parmesan may already provide enough seasoning. Serve immediately in warm bowls with the clams arranged shell-side up.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 498 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 12 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more consistent weeknight result, use 450 g frozen chopped clams with 120 ml bottled clam juice instead of live clams; add them after the wine has reduced for 3 minutes, then simmer for 4 minutes until hot. For a dairy-free version, omit the Parmesan and finish with 1 tbsp chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon; the brown-butter sauce remains glossy from emulsification with pasta water. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 1 day, reheating gently with a splash of water. Cooked seafood should reach 63°C internally; if reheating leftovers, bring the sauce to a full simmer and verify the temperature before serving.
