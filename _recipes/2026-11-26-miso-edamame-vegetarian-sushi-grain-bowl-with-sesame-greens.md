# Miso-Edamame Vegetarian Sushi Grain Bowl with Sesame Greens

Toasted rice, crisp cucumber, and edamame meet a glossy sesame-miso dressing in this balanced sushi bowl, with every bite layered and crunchy.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 180 g brown sushi rice
- 500 ml water
- 160 g shelled edamame
- 1 small cucumber
- 1 medium carrot
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 0.5 tsp lime juice
- 0.25 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar for the rice

## Method

1. Step 1: Rinse the brown rice under cold water for 30 seconds, rubbing the grains gently with your fingertips until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well and combine with 500 ml water in a small saucepan.
2. Step 2: Bring the rice to a boil, cover, and reduce the heat to the lowest setting. Cook for 20 minutes, then remove from the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. The grains should be tender with a slight chew; loosen them with a fork.
3. Step 3: While the rice rests, bring a second saucepan of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the edamame and cook for 4 minutes. Drain, cool briefly under cold running water, and remove the outer pods; the beans should be bright green and firm.
4. Step 4: Whisk the miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, lime juice, and chilli flakes in a small bowl with 2 tablespoons of water until glossy and smooth. Taste with a teaspoon of rice vinegar if the salt level needs balancing.
5. Step 5: Fold 1 tablespoon of the remaining rice vinegar into the warm rice. Season the spinach with a teaspoon of the dressing, then slice the cucumber into thin half-moons and shred the carrot into short ribbons.
6. Step 6: Divide the rice among four bowls. Arrange the spinach, cucumber, carrot, and edamame in separate sections, then fan the avocado over the grains. Spoon over the sesame-miso dressing and scatter with sesame seeds.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 16 g
- Carbohydrates: 59 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 11 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more pronounced sushi flavour, add a teaspoon of finely grated wasabi to the dressing, or replace half the rice vinegar with seasoned rice vinegar. Keep the dressing separate during meal prep and add it shortly before serving so the rice retains its grain texture; the assembled bowl will keep for up to 2 hours in the refrigerator, although the cucumber softens over time.
