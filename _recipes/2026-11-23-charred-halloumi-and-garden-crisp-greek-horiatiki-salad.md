# Charred Halloumi and Garden-Crisp Greek Horiatiki Salad

Crisp cucumber, juicy tomato, and oregano meet smoky, golden halloumi in this 25-minute Greek salad with a bright lemony finish.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 150 g halloumi, cut into 2 cm slices
- 1 medium cucumber, cut into 1.5 cm chunks
- 250 g ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 green bell pepper, cut into thin strips
- 60 g arugula
- 1/2 small flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the halloumi dry and cut it into 2 cm slices. Set a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and add 1 teaspoon of olive oil. Once the oil shimmers, arrange the cheese in a single layer and grill for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until deep golden with dark grill marks. Transfer to a plate lined with paper towel.
2. Step 2: While the halloumi cooks, combine the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and green bell pepper in a large bowl. Add the arugula and parsley, then season with the remaining olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, sea salt, and black pepper.
3. Step 3: Toss the vegetables gently with your hands, using broad folding motions so the cucumber stays crisp and the tomatoes do not collapse into a watery dressing. Taste and adjust the lemon juice and salt.
4. Step 4: Arrange the vegetables on two serving plates. Top each portion with the grilled halloumi and spoon a little of the seasoned tomato juices over the cheese. Serve immediately while the halloumi is still warm and the vegetables are cold and crunchy.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 21 g
- Carbohydrates: 14 g
- Fat: 32 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For the most pronounced grill flavor, dry the halloumi thoroughly before it meets the pan; moisture is the main obstacle to browning. Keep the heat medium-high rather than high, because halloumi can scorch before its center softens. The salad can be assembled up to 2 hours ahead, but add the halloumi just before serving so its crust remains crisp. If the tomatoes are especially juicy, drain their liquid before tossing and reserve it to dress the warm cheese.
