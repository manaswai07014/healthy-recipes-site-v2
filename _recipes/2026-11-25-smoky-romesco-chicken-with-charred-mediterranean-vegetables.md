# Smoky Romesco Chicken with Charred Mediterranean Vegetables

Charred peppers and zucchini gain smoky depth under the broiler, while a tangy almond romesco clings to tender chicken with a nutty, peppery finish.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 300 g boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 1 large red bell pepper
- 1 medium zucchini, cut into thick half-moons
- 150 g cherry tomatoes
- 60 g dry-roasted almonds
- 1 slice whole-grain bread, torn into pieces
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp fine salt, divided
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 10 g flat-leaf parsley
- 60 g baby spinach

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat the oven to 220°C. Place the bell pepper and cherry tomatoes on a lined tray, drizzle with 1 teaspoon of olive oil, and season with 1/4 teaspoon salt. Roast for 18-22 minutes, until the pepper skin wrinkles and blackens in spots and the tomatoes slump and release their juices.
2. Step 2: Meanwhile, season the chicken with the remaining salt and black pepper. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat with the remaining olive oil. Sear the chicken for 5-6 minutes without moving it, until a deep golden crust forms. Reduce the heat, add 60 ml water, cover, and cook for 8-10 minutes more. Verify the thickest point reaches 75°C, then rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
3. Step 3: Put the almonds and torn bread in a food processor and pulse to coarse crumbs. Add the garlic, vinegar, tomato paste, smoked paprika, and 2 tablespoons of water. Blend for 30 seconds, scraping down the bowl once.
4. Step 4: Remove the pepper stem and seeds, then add the roasted pepper and tomatoes to the processor with their juices. Blend for 45-60 seconds until the sauce is thick but spoonable. Add the parsley and pulse briefly, leaving visible flecks. Taste: the sauce should be roasted, tangy, and gently smoky; add a splash of water if it is too stiff.
5. Step 5: Set the oven to its broiler setting. Arrange the zucchini on a clean tray in a single layer and broil for 4-6 minutes, turning once, until browned at the edges but still slightly crisp. Keep the pan close to the heat and watch closely, as thin slices can scorch quickly.
6. Step 6: Divide the spinach between two plates. Arrange the sliced chicken and charred zucchini over the greens, spoon the romesco generously across the top, and finish with a small splash of sherry vinegar. Serve while the chicken is warm and the vegetables retain their blistered edges.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 498 kcal
- Protein: 48 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 21 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a hotter romesco, add 1/2 teaspoon cayenne with the paprika; for a smokier result, char the pepper under the broiler before roasting. The sauce keeps for 3 days refrigerated in an airtight container, and a splash of water or stock loosens it before serving. If preparing ahead, roast the vegetables and blend the romesco while the chicken cooks, then slice the chicken only after it has rested so the juices remain in the meat.
