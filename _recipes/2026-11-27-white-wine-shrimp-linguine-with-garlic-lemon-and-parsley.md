# White-Wine Shrimp Linguine with Garlic, Lemon and Parsley

Juicy shrimp sizzle in garlic and white wine while linguine turns glossy with lemon and parsley. A tender, briny sauce clings to every strand in 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 180 g linguine
- 300 g peeled, deveined shrimp with tails removed
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 60 ml low-sodium chicken or fish stock
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 20 g flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a vigorous boil. Add the linguine and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally so the strands separate. Before draining, reserve 120 ml of the starchy cooking water; the pasta should still have a slight resistance when tested.
2. Step 2: Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels. Heat the olive oil in a wide sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook for 60-90 seconds per side, just until opaque and firm; transfer them to a plate. The shells are not needed for this quick sauce.
3. Step 3: Lower the pan to medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking for 30-45 seconds until the garlic smells fragrant and turns pale gold but not brown. Pour in the white wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes, reducing by roughly half as the alcohol cooks off.
4. Step 4: Add the stock and 60 ml of the reserved pasta water. Simmer for 2 minutes, then return the shrimp to the pan. Add the drained linguine and toss vigorously for 1 minute, adding more pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce looks glossy and lightly coats the noodles.
5. Step 5: Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the lemon juice, parsley, salt and black pepper. Taste before adding extra salt because the shrimp and sauce may already be seasoned. Divide among four warm bowls and serve immediately, with extra parsley scattered over the top.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 9 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a saucier result, increase the stock to 90 ml and add another 30 ml of pasta water during tossing. The starch released from the pasta is what turns the wine reduction into a clingy sauce, so reserve more water than you think you need. Cook the shrimp separately to prevent them from becoming rubbery while the wine reduces.

This recipe was developed in-house by our editorial kitchen, drawing on Italian coastal cooking traditions and adapted for a healthy, weeknight table. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 1 day; loosen with a splash of stock when reheating over low heat. Cooked shrimp must reach an internal temperature of 63°C.
