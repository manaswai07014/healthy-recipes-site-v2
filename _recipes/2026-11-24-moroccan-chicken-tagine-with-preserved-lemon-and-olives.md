# Moroccan Chicken Tagine with Preserved Lemon and Olives

Chicken thighs simmer with caramelized onion, warm spices, and briny preserved lemon until tender, with bright herbs and olives added at the end.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp red chili flakes
- 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 240 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 1 preserved lemon, pulp discarded, rind finely sliced
- 80 g pitted green olives, halved
- 1 tbsp fresh mint leaves, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro leaves, chopped
- 200 g cooked whole-wheat couscous
- 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the chicken thighs dry and cut each into two even pieces. Season both sides with the salt and black pepper. This removes surface moisture so the meat browns rather than steams.
2. Step 2: Heat the olive oil in a wide, heavy saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring often, until softened and golden at the edges. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Step 3: Stir in the cumin, coriander, cinnamon, turmeric, and chili flakes. Toast the spices for 45-60 seconds, stirring continuously, until their aroma rises and the oil looks speckled with spice.
4. Step 4: Increase the heat to high. Arrange the chicken in a single layer and sear for 3-4 minutes without moving it, until deeply browned underneath. Turn and brown the second side for 2-3 minutes.
5. Step 5: Pour in the chicken stock and scrape the browned onion fragments from the base of the pan. Add the preserved lemon rind. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer, then cover and cook over low heat for 15 minutes.
6. Step 6: Check that the thickest pieces reach 75°C internally, using a food thermometer. If needed, continue cooking for another 3-5 minutes with the lid ajar. The chicken should be tender but still hold together when pressed.
7. Step 7: Add the olives and cook uncovered for 3 minutes. Remove from the heat and fold in the mint and cilantro. Taste the sauce before adding more salt, because the preserved lemon and olives are already salty.
8. Step 8: Spoon the cooked whole-wheat couscous onto four plates and divide the tagine over it. Finish each serving with a spoonful of Greek yogurt and a final scatter of herbs.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 476 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 34 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a thicker sauce, remove the lid after the chicken reaches 75°C and simmer for 5-8 minutes; the stock should reduce enough to lightly coat the meat. If the preserved lemon rind is very soft, slice it just before adding it so it retains some texture. The tagine keeps for up to 3 days in the refrigerator; cool it uncovered before sealing, then reheat gently with a splash of stock. The couscous can be prepared while the chicken simmers.
