# Weeknight Provençal Beef Daube with Chestnut Mushrooms

Beef shoulder becomes tender as it simmers in tomato, herbs, and chestnut mushrooms. Browning the meat first builds a deep savory sauce in one pot.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g lean beef shoulder, trimmed and cut into 3cm pieces
- 200g chestnut mushrooms, quartered
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium carrot, finely chopped
- 2 celery stalks, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 200ml dry red wine
- 200g crushed tomatoes
- 100ml water
- 1 tsp dried Provençal herbs
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the beef dry and season with the salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a heavy casserole over medium-high heat. Add the beef in two batches, leaving space between the pieces. Sear for 2-3 minutes per side until deeply browned, then transfer to a plate.
2. Step 2: Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion, carrot, and celery to the same pot. Cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables soften and the onions turn translucent. Scrape up the browned beef juices from the base.
3. Step 3: Stir in the garlic and tomato paste. Cook for 1 minute until the paste darkens slightly and smells toasted rather than raw. Add the flour and stir for 30 seconds to coat the vegetables.
4. Step 4: Pour in the red wine and scrape the bottom of the pot clean. Simmer for 3 minutes, reducing the wine by roughly half. Add the crushed tomatoes, water, Provençal herbs, and bay leaf, then return the beef to the pot.
5. Step 5: Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook for 20 minutes. The beef should yield easily to a spoon but still retain some texture. Add the mushrooms and cook uncovered for another 8-10 minutes, until the sauce thickens and coats the meat.
6. Step 6: Remove the bay leaf and let the daube stand for 5 minutes. Check that the beef is thoroughly cooked, with no pink remaining and juices running clear, then spoon the sauce and mushrooms over the meat.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a richer sauce without adding fat, simmer the uncovered stew for 5 minutes before serving so excess moisture evaporates. If the sauce becomes too thick, loosen it with a tablespoon of warm water. The daube keeps for 3 days in an airtight container in the refrigerator; gently reheat on the stove with a splash of water, stirring until the sauce moves freely around the pan.
